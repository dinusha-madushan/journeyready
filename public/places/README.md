# Destination Images for Dashboard

Add images for each destination to this folder.

## Required Images (12):

1. **sigiriya.jpg** - Sigiriya Rock Fortress
2. **ella.jpg** - Ella town with Nine Arches Bridge
3. **kandy.jpg** - Kandy Lake and Temple of the Tooth
4. **galle.jpg** - Galle Fort walls and lighthouse
5. **mirissa.jpg** - Mirissa beach and palm trees
6. **nuwara-eliya.jpg** - Tea plantations and hills
7. **colombo.jpg** - Colombo city and skyline
8. **trincomalee.jpg** - Trincomalee beaches
9. **yala.jpg** - Yala National Park wildlife
10. **anuradhapura.jpg** - Ancient stupas
11. **arugam-bay.jpg** - Arugam Bay surf beach
12. **jaffna.jpg** - Jaffna temples

## Image Specifications:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 1200x800px or similar aspect ratio (3:2)
- **File size**: Under 500KB (optimize for web)
- **Quality**: High quality, well-lit photos
- **Orientation**: Landscape (horizontal)

## Where to Find Images:

1. **Unsplash**: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/sri-lanka
2. **Pexels**: https://www.pexels.com/search/sri%20lanka/
3. **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/images/search/sri%20lanka/
4. Your own photos!

## Tips:

- Choose images that best represent each destination
- Ensure images are licensed for free use
- Optimize images before adding (use tinypng.com)
- Use descriptive, recognizable views
- Avoid overly dark or cluttered images

## Fallback:

If images are not added, the dashboard will show beautiful gradient backgrounds based on the destination type (Beach, Hills, History, Wildlife).
